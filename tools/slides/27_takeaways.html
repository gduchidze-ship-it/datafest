Takeaways
Not every agent decision deserves an LLM.
1
For every step, ask: writing or deciding?
Generation for new text and code. Typed answers for everything else.
2
The model returns probabilities. Your code makes the call.
predicate, choice and score, with thresholds set from your own labelled data.
3
Keep the LLM for the cases that need it.
At Impel that is about 10% of jailbreak checks, and the bill is 68% lower.
